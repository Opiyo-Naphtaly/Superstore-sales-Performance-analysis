## Key findings

- Shipping duration is roughly flat across order size buckets - order size isn't what's driving
  delivery speed, Ship Mode is.
- A meaningful chunk of the product catalog sits in the bottom 10% of sales and is worth reviewing
  for discontinuation or bundling.
- Revenue in every customer segment is concentrated in a small number of states rather than spread
  evenly - a classic 80/20 pattern.
- A couple of regions sit below the average regional revenue and are the stronger candidates for a
  targeted marketing campaign.
